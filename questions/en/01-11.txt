PROMPT: Present continuous is formed with am / is / are + verb-ing. Which form is correct? "I ___ a book right now."
RIGHT ANSWER: am reading
EXPLANATION: "I" takes "am" in present continuous. The verb "read" adds -ing: "am reading."
WRONG ANSWER: is reading
EXPLANATION: "Is" is used with he / she / it, not with "I." With "I," use "am."
WRONG ANSWER: are reading
EXPLANATION: "Are" is used with you / we / they, not with "I." With "I," use "am."
WRONG ANSWER: reading
EXPLANATION: The present continuous requires an auxiliary verb (am / is / are). "Reading" alone is incomplete.
